## New

- **Offline downloads** – recorded classes can be saved to the device from the class list. Downloads pause on cellular unless the learner allows it in settings.
- **Download manager** – one screen for everything saved, with storage used and a clear-all action.

## Improved

- **Player cold start** – the player now preloads the manifest while the class screen opens, cutting start time from about 2.5 s to under 1 s.
- **Resume position** – resumes at the last watched second across devices.

## Fixed

- Subtitles no longer disappear after seeking.
- Notes tab keeps scroll position when switching back from the player.

## Notes

Rolled out to 100% on November 2. Minimum iOS version is now 16.
